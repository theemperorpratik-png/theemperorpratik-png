## About me

I'm a full-stack developer who lives in **Node.js and MongoDB** and builds out to **React, Next.js, TypeScript, and SQL** from there. Most days I'm either deep in a MERN app or working with a friend on our web agency, building sites for small businesses that need to get online without the enterprise price tag.

- 🔭 Currently building — small business sites through my agency
- 🌱 Leveling up — Next.js and TypeScript, moving past "working knowledge" into "second nature"
- ⚡ Detour project — ESP32 robot car firmware, because sometimes software should talk to hardware
- 💬 Ask me about — Node/Express APIs, MongoDB schema design, or shipping fast for non-technical clients
- 📫 Reach me — see the bottom of this page
